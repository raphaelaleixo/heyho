The only thing measured here is how many times the sheet gets downloaded, alongside anonymous page views. No cookies, no accounts, nothing personal.

What's on the sheet

  • Spot it

    Festival style and concert scenes — colored hair, band shirts, the crowd putting on its own show. Tally each one up to five times.

  • Do it

    Eight special missions: invent a new dance move, high-five another kid, pick a landmark as home base, bin your rubbish.

  • Keep it

    The after-party page — the coolest outfit, the silliest thing you saw, the band name you'd pick, the one thing you never want to forget.

How to use it

Print it, hand it over with a pencil, and that's the whole setup.

No app to install, no account, nothing to charge, and it keeps working when the signal doesn't — which, at a festival, is most of the day.

There's a card at the top for a name, the festival and the date. Fill it in and the sheet stops being a printout and starts being something worth keeping.

Pro tip

Laminate it, or slide it into a plastic wallet. It survives a spilled drink, it's a firm surface to write on in a field, and a whiteboard marker wipes straight off — so one sheet lasts the whole season.

Why I made this

Going to events with my daughter made me realise how handy it is to have extra entertainment for the little ones.

If this activity made your festival easier, or your kids enjoyed it, I'd be thrilled to know.
